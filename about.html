Our house

One grill on the Marginal in 2013. Twelve houses in twelve countries today. Same coal, same long tables.

2013

2013 · Maputo

Twelve tables and a coal grill.

The first house opened on Avenida Marginal with one grill, one fryer and a menu that fit on a postcard. The Zambeziana chicken was on it from day one.

2016 · Cape Town & Lisbon

The recipes start to travel.

Two new houses, and a rule that still holds: every head chef spends a month cooking in another house before they open their own.

2019 · The bar & the cellar

A bar worth staying for.

We added a cocktail bar and a 320-label cellar. Dinner started finishing at 1 a.m.

2022 · Online

One kitchen, delivered.

Our ordering app launched in Maputo and Johannesburg. Delivery now makes up a third of every night's orders.

2026 · Twelve houses

Still the same long table.

Twelve houses, 34 nationalities on staff and around 9,000 plates a day. The coals are still lit at ten.

What we hold to

Serve, create, expand

Serve

Food goes out hot or it goes back. A plate waits at the pass for four minutes at most, and every server tastes the specials before service.

Create

Each house adds dishes from its own city. The best of them travel: the paella, the ramen and the Zambeziana chicken all started that way.

Expand

We open one house at a time and only when a head chef from an existing house is ready to lead it. No franchises.

Join us

Work with us, or eat with us more often

We hire cooks, servers and bartenders in every house. The community list gets first access to event tickets and new dishes.
