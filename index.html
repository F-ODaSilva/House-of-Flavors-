10:00 coals lit

It starts with real fire.

Every house cooks over coal. The grill master lights it at ten and it is ready by lunch. Nothing on the grill menu touches gas.

12 countries in the kitchen

Then forty pairs of hands.

Recipes travel between our houses. The Zambeziana chicken started in Maputo, the ramen broth in Kyoto, the paella with Joao in Spain.

4 min from pass to table

Checked twice at the pass.

Head chef Michelle Duvier tastes every sauce before service and every plate before it leaves. If it waits, it goes back.
